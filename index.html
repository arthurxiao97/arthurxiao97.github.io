Research Projects

2021 — 2025
University of Michigan

Nitride micro-LEDs

I designed and synthesized novel heterostructures to improve the efficiency of red-emitting InGaN micro-LEDs from 3% to over 10%. I also nanofabricated the first integration of nitride micro-LEDs on silicon. More in the media.

2019 — 2021
University of Michigan

Nitrides for artificial photosynthesis

I studied and clarified crucial mechanisms by which III-nitride nanowires split water efficiently.

2017 — 2019
Amherst College

Bose-Einstein condensates

I experimentally realized, for the first time, the entire family of spin-2 magnetic phases in bulk condensate as well as in vortex cores.
