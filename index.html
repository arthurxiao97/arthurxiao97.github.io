Yixin Xiao, Ph.D.
About Research Publications Contact

Yixin Xiao (Ph.D. '25, University of Michigan) specializes in semiconductor materials research and development. More specifically, he has 5+ years of cleanroom nanofabrication and epitaxy experience. He has led the development of the highest efficiency nitride-based red-emitting micro-LEDs, paving the way for full RGB micro-LED displays entirely in a single material system (i.e. the nitrides), which would entail many advantages over the micro-LED displays currently avaiable.
